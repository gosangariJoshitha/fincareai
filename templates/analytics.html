Help & Support SLAs

FinCare AI operates an automated multi-agent support workflow adhering to strict turnaround regulations.

P2 High (Declined POS / Transfer) Within 24 Hours
P3 Standard (General queries) Within 48 Hours
P4 Low (Account statements) Within 72 Hours
